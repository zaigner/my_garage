### Rejected after verification

- **EC12:** a concurrent item delete during activation causes a deferred FK failure. It needs an owner deleting an item at the very moment they activate. maybe-false, and low if true.
- **EC13:** the issued-link cookie survives a redirect. It is path-scoped, httponly, a browser-session cookie, and `max_age` is enforced on read.
- **EC15:** JS vs Python name normalisation differs on exotic whitespace. Rare, and the outcome is only a missing or extra confirm.
- **EC8:** the issued page can show an already-claimed URL during its 10-minute window. Cosmetic.
- **ADV16:** `compare_digest` after a hash lookup is redundant. Carried from 3.4 triage B8 (it mirrors `verify_executor_code`; the AC requires it).
- **ADV14:** reversing migration 0022 deletes itemless snapshots. The old schema can't hold them; this is documented in the migration and required by Story 3.1.
- **EC11 / ADV8:** `ESTATE_VIEW_EVENTS` is dead. It is used by `test_event_groups_cover_every_event_type` (`test_estate_access_service.py:195`) as the event-group completeness check, and Story 4.3 is its reader.
- **ADV12** (snapshots don't record the designation at activation) is not a defect. It is a scope question for Epic 4: Q5.

## Open questions

- **Q1.** Should claimed beneficiary access have an absolute end (for example, when the estate is settled, or N days after the claim), or keep sliding until revoked (F10)?
- **Q2.** Should the owner be told that item notes and custom fields are visible to the designated beneficiary, or get per-item control (F9)?
- **Q3.** Should a failed claim on a known link log `LINK_REJECTED`, as executor codes log `CODE_REJECTED` (F12)? Story 3.4 chose "log nothing".
- **Q4.** Should the pre-claim page leave out the owner's name (F11)?
- **Q5.** For Epic 4's PDFs, should the activation snapshot also record each item's designation at activation, so later reassignment is visible (ADV12)?

### Decisions (Zaigner77, 2026-10-29)

- **Q1:** keep sliding. Claimed access lasts until the executor revokes it or the code changes. No change.
- **Q2:** add a hint on the item form saying notes and custom fields are visible to the item's designated beneficiary after activation. No per-item control.
- **Q3:** yes. A failed claim on a URL that matches a real link logs `LINK_REJECTED`; unknown URLs still log nothing.
- **Q4:** keep the owner's name on the claim page and add `noindex` (F11).
- **Q5:** decide in Epic 4 planning; recorded on Epic 4 in `epics-estate-legacy.md`. **Decided in Epic 4 planning (2026-10-29): record each designation at activation.** The activation snapshot gains the item's designation, and the executor PDF flags items reassigned since (Story 4.2 in `epics-estate-legacy.md`).

Q2, Q3 and Q4 ship in their own PR after the item 7 refactor. Items 8 (minus `noindex`, which Q4 now covers), 9 and 10 were done on 2026-10-29.
